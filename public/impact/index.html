About

CalClaim was created by concerned and caring citizens of California who want to make sure their friends and communities can access the help they need.

This project started out as a local personal project in Oakland, then launched formally to expand and serve all of California.

Privacy

We can’t see your phone number or answers. We only see a unique id and whether you got through CalClaim successfully or ran into issues. We use that to detect problems and improve the system. We only save your preferences if you opt in to further messages; otherwise, type ERASE to the CalClaim messenger bot in Telegram to delete your information anytime.

Full privacy policy and disclaimer

What we collect

  • Answers you choose while finding programs (household size, income band, ZIP when needed for county programs, offer actions)
  • Your Application Guide items derived from our program list
  • Optional free-form text or voice notes you send as alpha feedback
  • Aggregate impact events: QR scans, friend-share and partner-link clicks, apply-page opens, follow-throughs (friend-share links use anonymous codes – not names)
  • Coarse location only (poster sites and city-level IP for the map – never street addresses)
  • Optional email and comments you submit on the contact form
  • Organization name, work email, and optional city if you sign up as a community partner (to send your QR kit and show your status page)
  • Street + city typed for the optional PG&E shut-off check is looked up once (US Census standardizer + PG&E map), then discarded – we keep only yes/no, never the street address, and we do not use that address for marketing, profiling, or anything else
  • If you give, the gift amount, whether it is monthly, and Stripe’s payment id – not your card or bank numbers

What we do not collect

  • Required document uploads (ID, pay stubs, bills)
  • Card numbers, bank account numbers, or PayPal passwords (optional gifts are processed by Stripe and PayPal; we do not store them)
  • Marketing email lists
  • Precise GPS unless you choose to share a location
  • Immigration status answers (asked only when needed – not stored, not linked to your phone number)
  • Stored street addresses – CalClaim does not keep your street address or use it for marketing or profiling; the optional PG&E shut-off check discards it after a one-time lookup

How we use data

We use your data anonymously to run your session, send your Application Guide and reminders, improve the demo UX, and publish aggregate impact metrics on the public dashboard. Partner signup details are used to email your QR kit and credit scans on your status page. Optional gifts are processed by Stripe and PayPal; we do not store card or bank numbers. We do not give or sell your data. We do not use your street address for anything beyond the optional one-time shut-off check that discards it.

Your choices

  • Type 'stop' – pause deadline reminders (keeps your data for your reference)
  • Type 'erase' – delete your CalClaim session data
  • Sign in from the partner leaderboard, then download the data shown on your status page (CSV of the metrics, charts, and map on that page)
  • Sign in from the partner leaderboard, then delete your account from your status page – we’ll warn you that all your partner information will be removed
