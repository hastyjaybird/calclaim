# CalClaim Privacy Policy (demo)

**Product:** CalClaim v2 — California financial aid / benefits navigator (Telegram)  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-31

## What we collect

- Your Telegram user id  
- Answers you tap (gate, household size, income band, offer actions)  
- Next-steps todo items and deadlines derived from our frozen program corpus  
- Free-form text you type that is not a recognized button/command (for developer quality control)  

## What we do **not** collect (v2)

- Document uploads (ID, pay stubs, bills)  
- Payment information  
- Marketing email lists  

## How we use data

- To run your session and send next-steps / reminder messages  
- To improve the demo UX via quiet QC logs (`data/responses.jsonl`)  

## Retention & deletion

- Message **STOP** or use **Help → Erase all my data** to delete your session, todos, reminder flags, and your QC log rows.  
- Hosting operators may wipe the demo database when the demo ends.

## Not affiliated

Not affiliated with PG&E, DHCS, CDSS, USDA, FCC, IRS, or any agency. Estimates only — not tax, legal, or benefits advice.
